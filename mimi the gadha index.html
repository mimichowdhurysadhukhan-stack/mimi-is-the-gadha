Mimi the Gadha Donkey

Mimi is a gadha, a donkey so bold, With thoughts that wander and stories retold. She brays her opinions loud and clear, Even when nobody asked to hear. She trots through life with a stubborn grin, Always convinced that she’s sure to win. Left turn, right turn—she’ll choose the wall, Trip on nothing, yet never fall. Wise like a rock, sharp as hay, Mimi argues night and day. A donkey’s crown sits proud on her head, Gadha by title, by actions well-spread. But laugh we must, for without her flair, Life would be quieter… and less fun to stare. So here’s to Mimi, the gadha divine— A donkey legend, one of a kind 🫏✨
— A playful poem
